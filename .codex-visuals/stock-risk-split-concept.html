How these claims pay at expiry

This mockup follows the original Risk Split V1 contract, with floor parameter $435.37 and cap parameter $653.05. Below $435.37, Protected receives all stock value and Upside receives zero. From $435.37 to $870.74, Protected receives $435.37 and Upside receives the rest. From $870.74 to $1,306.10, each receives half. Above $1,306.10, Protected receives $653.05 and Upside receives the rest. These are values used to determine the fractions of escrowed MSFTc delivered, not guaranteed dollar payments. The labels “floor” and “cap” name contract parameters; they are not a minimum return or a stock-price ceiling.
