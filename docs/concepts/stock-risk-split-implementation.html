The fixing must be verifiable

Open→Awaiting fixing→Settled

At expiry, minting stops. A complete pair can still withdraw one stock token. Once the precommitted feed observation is final, either side can redeem alone for its fraction.

Launch gate. Reading latestRoundData() whenever a caller decides to finalize lets them choose the timing. The settlement adapter needs a fixed observation rule, historical proof or challenge window, and a documented missing-feed fallback.

A B20 total-return feed already includes the issuer multiplier. Do not apply it again when computing the claim fractions.

Two visible limit-order books

Users post bids and asks for each claim in USDC. A matching engine pairs compatible orders; a settlement contract transfers tokens and USDC.

Post and cancel

A user signs a price, size and expiry for a Protected or Upside bid or ask. Unfilled size can be cancelled.

signed limit order → visible book

Match and settle

The matcher executes a crossed bid and ask at an authorized price. Partial fills leave the remaining size on the book.

offchain match → onchain transfer

Mint / recombine

One stock token mints both claims; a matched claim pair returns the stock token. Arbitrage links both books to stock value.

Protected + Upside ⇄ stock

The app must show best bid/ask, depth, recent trades and each user's open orders. Resting orders provide liquidity; no AMM LP pool is required. The two claim prices sum toward the moving stock-token price after costs, not a fixed $1.
